## 1. Who NZA is, in one paragraph

NZA — Net Zero Advisory — is a UK consultancy that **decodes decarbonisation for the built environment**. The practice sits in the gap between conventional MEP/building-physics consultancies (who speak the building) and energy/market consultancies (who speak the grid and tariffs): NZA holds all three languages at once — building physics, grid mechanics, energy markets — and combines them with bespoke digital tools (PABLO, NZ:AI, decodED) and a strategic, partnership-based approach. NZA's deliverables are **assets, not deliverables**: every engagement leaves the client with a tool they keep using — a digital twin, a living dashboard, a co-built platform — so each engagement makes both the client and the broader NZA toolkit stronger.

The work runs through three lenses applied to every engagement: **data, digital tools, and strategy** — rigorous analysis, made usable, turned into decisions.

> **[CONFIRM]** The positioning sentence — *"We decode decarbonisation for the built environment."* This is the locked headline and the single line that anchors the rest. Confirm or replace.

## 2. The feeling

Five adjectives, in order of priority:

- **Engineered** — the work is structural. The voice should sound like an engineer who can write — confident, technically literate, allergic to fluff.
- **Connected** — net zero in the built environment isn't one discipline; it's a system. The brand should feel like one practice working a whole connected system, not a menu of services.
- **Compounding** — the work is long-term partnership, not project. Reports become tools; tools become platforms; platforms become co-built. Each engagement makes the next one stronger.
- **Quietly confident** — claims are direct, unhedged. Specific over general. The design carries that without shouting.
- **Editorial** — the site should feel like an architectural document, not a marketing page. Generous whitespace. Hairline rules. One clear idea per screen.

---

## 3. What NZA is NOT

- **Not corporate-blue-consultancy.** No stock photography, no beige sans-serif, no "delivering value at scale".
- **Not eco-startup-green-with-leaves.** No leaf icons, no oversaturated greens, no Earth-from-space imagery.
- **Not playful-illustrated-explainer.** No mascots, no flat illustrated characters, no soft-sci-fi gradient blobs.
- **Not maximalist data-viz.** Numbers are everywhere in the work; the design uses them sparingly. Restraint over density.
- **Not aspirational marketing copy.** *"Pioneering," "reimagining," "unlocking," "bespoke insights," "tailored solutions," "industry-leading expertise"* — banned.
- **Not a software product company.** The products serve the practice, not the other way around.

## 4. The audiences

| Surface | Audience | Tonal centre |
|---|---|---|
| **Website** | Procurement / sustainability / estates directors at universities, real-estate developers, contractors, MEP consultancies. Plus sustainability peers in the consulting market | Editorial-confident. Engineering register. Marketing without sounding like marketing. |

## 5. The signature move

**Italic-on-key-word as the typographic signature.** A single emphasised word in DM Serif Display italic, rendered in coral, set inside a Stolzl Light headline. Used once per major heading. The italicised word is *the word that does the work* — the contrarian word, the word being redefined, the word the headline pivots on.

Examples in locked copy:

- *decode* in the hero — *"We decode decarbonisation for the built environment."*
- *discipline* in Capabilities — *"Net zero in the built environment isn't one discipline."*
- *one* in Approach — *"Six capabilities, one approach."*
- *platforms* in Co-built platforms — the disruptive sixth card

The serif italic at 1.10–1.13em above the surrounding Stolzl Light, in a single locked coral, is the typographic fingerprint of the practice. Everything else — surfaces, palette, motion — holds back so this lands.

## 6. The narrative ambition

The story NZA tells visually is **the connected, system-level nature of decarbonisation in the built environment** — that the answer is only right when you hold building physics, energy systems, supply chains, climate exposure and operations together.

Two narrative spines:

1. **Connected system.** Buildings sit inside energy systems, supply chains, climate exposure and the people who use them. NZA works the whole system. The Capabilities screen carries this: a connected-system diagram with whole-building approach at the centre and energy-systems / wider-context as concentric arcs around it. The diagram is being drawn bespoke by Chris in Illustrator.

2. **Three lenses, six capabilities.** Every engagement runs through *data, digital tools, strategy*. Those lenses cut across six capability areas (whole-estate strategy, smart energy strategy, behind-the-meter strategy, carbon accounting & pathways, climate resilience, co-built platforms). The Approach screen is built around a 3×2 grid where each card click-to-reveals its three-lens detail in an inset panel between the rows.

The site's structural rhythm is **paper / navy alternation across five screens** — Home navy, Capabilities navy, Approach paper, Products navy, Clients paper.

## 7. Hard constraints — the locked system

These are non-negotiable. Anything in the research output that contradicts these isn't usable.

### Type

- **Stolzl** — display, headlines, eyebrows, navigation, button labels. Light (300) for major headlines, Book (400) for body-context display, Medium (500) for small caps and eyebrow labels.
- **Söhne** — body, descriptions, captions, stats, anything 13–17px running text. Light (300) / Regular (400) / Medium (500). **Inter Tight** is the working substitute until Söhne is licensed.
- **DM Serif Display** — *exclusively* for italic emphasis words inside Stolzl headlines. Never upright, never body, only italic, only inside a headline, only one or two words at a time. **Always coral.** **Always bumped 1.10–1.13em** above the surrounding font-size to align cap-heights with Stolzl Light.

### Colour

- **`--paper` `#FAF7F0`** — primary cream paper background.
- **`--cream` `#F5F1E8`** — secondary cream for cards on paper.
- **`--ink` `#1A1D20`** — near-black body copy on cream.
- **`--ink-navy` `#0E1120`** — deep navy for inverted sections.
- **`--coral` `#F75A55`** — *the* coral. Not a gradient, not a range. **Reserved for moments**: italic emphasis, the 24px coral rule before eyebrows, the 32px coral rule under capability titles, small accent dots, the active-state underline, the radial glow on the disruptive Co-built platforms card. **Never** a button fill, **never** a card or section background, **never** a heading colour outside italic emphasis.

### Three lens accents (taxonomic only)

These distinguish *Data / Digital tools / Strategy* across the site. Restrained — used as small dots and tag colour, never as card backgrounds or major chrome.

- `--lens-data` `#2F4C6B` — deep navy-blue
- `--lens-tools` `#7A5DAE` — purple (also PABLO's product accent)
- `--lens-strategy` `#3a7d7e` — NZA teal

On navy surfaces these brighten for legibility (data → `#8AA0C0`, tools → `#B79DD9`, strategy → `#6EAEA9`).

### Screen rhythm

Five screens — Home, Capabilities, Approach, Products, Clients — alternating navy / paper. **One screen per section.** If a screen exceeds one viewport, that's the cue to compress or use the click-to-reveal pattern.

### Other

- **8 px spacing grid.** All radii, gaps, paddings derive from it.
- **Hairline rules** (1px, ~10% black on cream / ~14% white on navy) over container backgrounds. Sections separate by hairlines and whitespace, not coloured panels.
- **Coral rules** as section markers: 24px before eyebrows, 32px under capability titles.
- **Iconography is Lucide** as the working default. NZA may build custom later.
- **No emoji. No stock photography. No leaf / sun / wind / Earth icons. No gradients.**

## 8. What's changed since the v1 brief

Surfacing this so it's clear which earlier directions are *closed*:

- **Pink → coral.** "NZA pink `#EE7C7B`" is gone. The signature colour is locked coral `#F75A55`.
- **Playfair Display → DM Serif Display.** Italic-only, coral-only, headline-only.
- **Five-gradient palette → no gradients.** The signature is the italic word, not a gradient.
- **Body face is now Söhne (Inter Tight as fallback).** Stolzl is no longer used at body sizes.
- **Ten scrolling screens → five distinct screens** with paper/navy alternation.
- **Assembly-stack diagram → connected-system circular diagram** for the Capabilities screen, drawn bespoke.
- **6-up capability grid (rejected in v1) is back** — but only on the Approach screen, with a click-to-reveal interaction that opens an inset panel between the rows. The interaction is what saves it from generic-consultancy territory.

## 10. What we want back

A **research deliverable**, not visual concepts. The locked system answered most of what the v1 brief was asking — what remains is genuinely open work. In rough priority:

1. **Motion principles** — 3–5 named patterns with rationale.
   The hero has a locked sequenced reveal (six layers, ~2.2s, then holds — see §7 of the locked design system). The Capabilities diagram has a locked sequenced reveal too. **What other named motion patterns serve the rest of the site?** ("Held reveal" is taken; "horizon hold," "evidence accumulation," something else?) Each principle: what it does, when to use it, why it serves *engineered / connected / compounding*.

2. **Density-without-losing-identity layout patterns.**
   The site is editorial and low-density. PABLO is a working tool — 30+ data elements on a panel. Reports sit between. **What's the shared grammar that holds across all three?** Is it density rules? A recurring diagrammatic element? A specific hierarchy pattern? Pin this down where the brief has been hand-waving.

3. **Infographic vocabulary beyond the scope landscape and the connected-system diagram.**
   Two are in hand: the GHG Protocol scope landscape (already specified for the hero) and the connected-system circular diagram (being drawn for Capabilities). NZA will need at least three more concept-types: **pathways over time, climate risk overlays, energy-system flows, building-portfolio rollups.** Propose 3–5 directions and say which suits which surface.

4. **Products page concept (NZ:AI · PABLO · decodED).**
   Currently undrafted. Should be navy canvas. The disruptive sixth card on Approach is the bridge into this page. How does this page introduce three products without becoming a software company's product page? What's the unifying frame that makes them feel like extensions of the practice, not a separate product line?

5. **Clients page concept.**
   Currently undrafted. Should be paper canvas, calm, evidence-led, work-as-the-hero. Logos, selected work, case-study entry points. How does it earn the calmness — what stops it being a generic logo wall?

6. **Mobile responsive behaviour.**
   The locked system is desktop-first. Specifically: how does the connected-system diagram work on mobile? The 3×2 Approach grid? The italic-on-key-word headline (cap-height bump behaves differently at small sizes)?

7. **For each item above: the reasoning** — why does this serve NZA specifically, tied back to the adjectives in §2 and the narrative in §6? Not "here are five motion patterns we like." The *why* is the deliverable.

**Format:** written report, diagrams welcome.
**Length:** thorough, not exhaustive — 10–15 pages.
